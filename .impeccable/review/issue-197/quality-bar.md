# Issue 197 quality bar

Operate, within the existing Fieldhouse Wayfinding system. The approved Roster beside context comp
sets the topology: compact campaign/season/lifecycle/dates/scale and full native Route Markers precede
independent readiness; a wide discovery-and-roster board sits beside a narrower participant context.
The first viewport must expose meaningful rows and participant evidence at working scale.

Preserve native system fonts, global Nova navigation, semantic theme colors, flat working boards,
visible keyboard focus and at least 44px controls. Mobile keeps discovery usable and participant
context becomes a modal sheet with focus containment, Escape and return focus. Local campaign
outcomes, effective season placement/source and eligibility remain distinct; Closed evidence is
immutable and archived records remain legible. Every existing filter/sort and note/tag action survives.

Generated sample records/counts are synthetic. The recorded contract corrects the generated route
captions and red Needs-placement text to product language and semantic amber. It preserves the
existing global navigation and system typography rather than adopting generated substitutions.
